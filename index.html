20+ YEARS OFTECHNICAL EXCELLENCE

ISHINGA PRODUCTIONS is a premier technical production company based in Durban, South Africa. We specialize in transforming events into unforgettable experiences through cutting-edge sound, lighting, and video technology. We offer end-to-end technical production management services. Our expertise spans musicals, corporate productions, festivals, stage management, and comprehensive event consultations.
